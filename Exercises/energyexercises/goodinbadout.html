Good in Bad out (Chi Kung)

Stand with feet about a shoulder-width apart. Bring the palms of the hands forward with the backs of the hand facing each other. Gather energy as you bring the palms back to the kidneys rising on the toes. Store the good energy in the kidneys; make fists as you gather bad energy from the liver and spleen. Then shoot the bad energy out with a sound (“shoo”) toward the floor as your heels return with a thud to the ground.
