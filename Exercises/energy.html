Energy Exercises

The Energy of Rising/ Falling Energy Sphere Good In Bad Out

Ways of listening can be discovered by doing these energy (Chi) exercises. These exercises are for building and storing energy in the body. The exercises are drawn from Chi Kung, T’aI Chi, Yoga and kinetic awareness practices and are modified for Deep Listening practice. Doing these exercises before sitting for listening or for studying or working can be helpful. If one is depleted, the exercises will help to restore energy in the body. Otherwise the exercises are very helpful to calm and balance mental activity.
