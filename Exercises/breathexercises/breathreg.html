Breath Regulation

Inhale through the nose for the count of 6. Hold for the count of 4. Keep the throat relaxed. Exhale through a small aperture of the lips with a sub vocalization of the syllable ‘hahhhh’ for the count of 8. Relax and wait for the count of 4 and repeat the cycle. Sub vocalization of ‘hahhhh’ restricts the epiglottis and helps direct energy to the lower abdomen.
