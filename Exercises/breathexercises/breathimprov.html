Breath Improvisation

Standing in a circle, bring your attention to your breath. With short or longer puffs of air use the sound of breaths to improvise a playful piece interacting with others for three to five minutes. Try not to vocalize the breaths. Notice any differences that you feel after the improvisation. Also reflect on the rhythms, texture and shape of the improvisation as if it were a composition.
